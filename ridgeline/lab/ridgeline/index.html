Ridgeline

Upload a photo of a skyline. Ridgeline traces the silhouette, breaks it into the sine waves that compose it, and shows the mountain assembling itself one frequency at a time — as a print or as a video of arms spinning it into being.

01 — upload

Give it a skyline

Works best with a clear silhouette against open sky — dusk shots and backlit ridgelines separate cleanest.

Choose a photo or drag one here
02 — trace the silhouette

Find the line

Ridgeline scans each column of the photo for where sky meets land. Adjust the threshold until the traced line (amber) hugs the real ridge.

03 — decompose

Choose an output

Panels are ordered by wave magnitude — the biggest, most structural waves appear first, smaller ones refine the shape after. The last panel overlays the full reconstruction on your original photo.

Arm count is chosen automatically — enough waves to carry 95% of the signal's energy.
