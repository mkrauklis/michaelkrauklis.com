The Lab

Small browser-based tools — each one a self-contained piece of applied math or simulation, open source and free to use.

Ridgeline

Traces a mountain silhouette from a photo, decomposes it with a discrete Fourier transform, and renders the reconstruction as an image sequence or animation.

Afterimage

Reconstructs an uploaded photo from a 256-number impression, using a small neural network trained once and shipped frozen — the reconstruction happens entirely in your browser.
