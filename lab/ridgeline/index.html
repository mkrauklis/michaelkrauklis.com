See how the math builds it — step-by-step panels, formulas, and an epicycle animation

Each step shows the equation directly: the ridge so far, plus the next wave ordered by magnitude — biggest and most structural first — equals the new ridge. Formulas are in terms of x ∈ [0, 1], the normalized position across the ridge. The number of steps is how many waves make it into the final result — the last step overlays whatever you land on onto your original photo, so you can see how close it gets.

Each step reveals the wave being added (top strip, red) before it merges into the ridge (green).

Ridgeline runs entirely in your browser — no photo is uploaded to a server. Part of The Lab by Michael Krauklis.
